Memento Mori shows your life as a grid of weeks. Every filled cell is a week you have lived, every empty one a week still ahead of you. It is not meant to frighten you. It is a quiet reminder that your time is limited, so you can spend it on what matters to you.

Your life at a glance
• See your life in weeks, months or years, zoom in and tap any cell to see when it was
• Other layouts if you like: spiral, liquid progress or tree rings
• A short quote every day and the next birthday coming up

How many years to plan with?
• The estimate starts from the UN World Population Prospects 2024 life tables for your country, age and sex
• Five optional lifestyle questions (smoking, activity, alcohol, weight, feeling connected) make it more personal
• It is calculated on your device. It is a statistical average, not a prediction, and you can set your own number at any time

Compass
• A gentle question for each week
• Life phases: see which phase you are in and how much of your lifespan it takes; add earlier phases if you like
• Intentions for this phase, to get done or to enjoy: at most three for "Now"; everything else can wait under "Someday"
• Close a phase consciously and decide what to take along and what to let go
• After you complete something, a quiet moment to take it in: how long it took, where it sits in your life and the time still ahead of you, with an optional note of gratitude
• Keep the moments you have lived and see them marked on your life grid
• Your life grid in the colours of your phases, in the other layouts too
• A look back at what you have lived
• No streaks, no scores, no pressure

People
• Keep the birthdays of friends and family, with groups, avatars, search and sorting
• Import birthdays from your contacts or a vCard file in a few taps, also without a year; it happens on your device and only the people you choose are saved
• Birthday reminders on the day and, if you like, the day before
• Optional yearly birthday events in your device calendar
• Together: both lives on one timeline and a rough count of how often you might still meet at your usual pace, with a way to plan time together

On your home screen and your wrist
• Home-screen widgets in three sizes; the large one shows your week grid
• A Wear OS app with a tile and a watch face complication (syncing with the watch needs the Google Play version)

And also
• An optional Sunday evening reminder: weekly with the week's question, or every 4 weeks with a look at your phase
• Two calm styles, Stoic and Warm, in light and dark, plus optional grid colours
• Backup and restore as a JSON file, including avatars
• English, German, French and Spanish

Your data stays with you
• Everything is stored on your device
• Your contacts are only read when you import birthdays, and only the people you choose are kept
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere
